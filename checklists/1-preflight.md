# Preflight checklist

Complete before design and build. Output: a signed [`project-brief.md`](../templates/project-brief.md), a prioritized [`url-map.csv`](../templates/url-map.csv), and accounts owned by the client.

## A · Strategy & research

- [ ] STR-01 Project brief signed off
- [ ] STR-02 Audiences based on evidence
- [ ] STR-03 Positioning statement
- [ ] STR-04 Business and search competitors analyzed
- [ ] STR-05 Keyword and intent research mapped to URLs
- [ ] STR-06 SERP analysis for money queries
- [ ] STR-07 Content gaps prioritized
- [ ] STR-08 Site-type profile applied
- [ ] STR-10 Baseline captured *(existing site)*
- [ ] STR-11 Market constraints *(multilingual)*
- [ ] SEO-34 Full URL inventory *(relaunch)*

## B · Content, UX & design foundations

- [ ] CON-02 Content briefs for launch pages
- [ ] UX-01 Information architecture validated
- [ ] DES-01 `DESIGN.md` drafted
- [ ] DES-02 `tokens.json` set up
